Meals for older people

We're launching meals for older people: daily meals cooked the same morning, for older people living alone or who no longer want or are able to cook every day.

They can be collected in a container or, depending on the case, we can bring them round.

It's a service the neighbourhood had been asking us for, and it fits exactly what we are: we do it because it's needed.
