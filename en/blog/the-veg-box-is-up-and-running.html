The veg box is up and running

Today we launched one of the projects that had been going round our heads for days: the canteen's sustainable veg box.

It was made possible by the families who wanted sustainable fruit and vegetables with a real social commitment behind them, and by the agroecology projects we'll be working alongside.

Who we're working with

De Peus a Terra, the Catalan project of the CEHDA association, which Cesc introduced us to.

And Conreu Sereny, which Xavi told us all about. Their fields are worth seeing.

Write to us and we'll explain exactly how the box works.
