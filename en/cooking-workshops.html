Cooking workshops with wine pairing

Seasonal cooking workshops for children, young people, families, groups of friends and companies. At our place on Carrer Mossèn Juliana or wherever you tell us.

Call us Email us

Types of workshop

  • Workshops for children, also run in cycles of several sessions as a cooking school
  • Cooking for young people: a dedicated workshop for learning to cook and fend for yourself
  • Family cooking: adults and children cooking together
  • Singles and groups of friends: cooking and eating together, whether you already know each other or you're meeting new people
  • Paella team building for companies
  • Bespoke workshops for organisations, schools and work teams
  • Seasonal one-offs: what to do with what the market has right now
  • Vegetarian cooking and pulses: plant protein without the boredom
  • Preserving and using everything up: stretching the season, wasting nothing

What they're like

You cook; you don't watch someone cook. Everyone gets their own tools and ingredients. At the end you eat what you made, sitting down and unhurried, which is the part that usually gets forgotten.

You leave with the recipes written down and, where it makes sense, a handout on the subject of the workshop.

Paella team building

One paella for the whole team, made by the team. Tasks get shared out, there's chopping, sautéing and waiting together, which is when people actually talk. At the end everyone eats what they've cooked, sitting down and unhurried.

It works well with large groups and needs less space than you'd think: an outdoor area or a courtyard is enough. We can run it at our place or wherever you're meeting.

At your place

We'll bring the workshop wherever suits: a school, a community centre, an organisation's premises or a private flat. We need a kitchen or, at minimum, water, tables and power. We bring the rest.

Questions about the workshops

Do I need to know how to cook?

No. The workshops work whether you have never held a knife or you cook every day.

Are ingredients included?

Yes. The price always covers all the ingredients and the equipment.

Do you run workshops for companies and organisations?

Yes. We adapt them to the group, the space and the goal. The format most requested is paella team building, but we also run seasonal one-offs and training on sustainable food.

How many people fit in a paella team building session?

It works well with large groups, and we can run it at our place or wherever you're meeting. Tell us how many you are and where, and we'll tell you what we need.

Can I bring my daughter?

To the family workshops, yes, and that's the whole point. For the adult one-offs we'd rather you didn't, because the pace is different.

Do we eat during the workshop?

Always. What gets cooked gets eaten at the end.

Tell us what you need

Call or email us. We reply with a real proposal and a fixed price, with no obligation and no endless forms.
