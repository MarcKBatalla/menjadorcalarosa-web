Farmers' market at Plaça de Masadas

Direct sales in the neighbourhood, with no middlemen. The person who grows it sells it, and the person who buys it knows their name.

Call us Email us

Why a farmers' market

Between the field and the table there are usually four or five hands taking a cut. The grower ends up with a fraction of the final price and the shopper has no idea where anything came from. A farmers' market removes that stretch: the producer sells directly, is paid what the work is worth, and answers in person for how it was grown.

For us it is the same idea as the canteen, applied to a whole neighbourhood.

What you'll find

  • Seasonal fruit and vegetables from nearby market gardens
  • Small-batch preserves and jams
  • Juices with no added sugar
  • Rescued varieties such as the ganxet bean
  • Produce from the same growers who supply our kitchen

Who sells here

The same projects that supply us: Conreu Sereny, De Peus a Terra, Cal Roio and other growers from the Vallès, the Maresme and the Penedès. You can ask them directly about how they farm, which varieties they plant, and why some things simply don't arrive in a given month.

Want a stall?

If you are a local producer interested in selling at the market, get in touch. We prioritise small-scale agroecological projects with a social commitment.

Questions about the market

How often is the market held?

Dates are set season by season. We announce them on Instagram and by email; write to us and we'll let you know about the next one.

Can I sell my produce there?

If you're a local producer, get in touch. We prioritise small-scale agroecological projects with a social commitment.

Do I need to be a co-operative member to shop there?

No. The market is open to everyone, local or not.

Tell us what you need

Call or email us. We reply with a real proposal and a fixed price, with no obligation and no endless forms.
