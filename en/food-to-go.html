Food to take to work

The lunchbox service: sustainable food cooked the same morning, so you don't have to choose between eating well and eating quickly.

Call us Email us

What it is

A lot of people working around here end up eating whatever, because there's no time to cook and the set lunch menu gets expensive every single day. The lunchbox service is the straightforward answer: the same kitchen that feeds the children's canteen, packaged to take away.

It is cooked the same morning. Not chilled, not pre-cooked and reheated.

How it works

  • Order by the week or by the day, whatever suits
  • Collect from the kitchen at Carrer de Mossèn Juliana, 23
  • Local delivery when the volume allows
  • Bring your own container and save the packaging
  • Seasonal menus, mostly vegetarian

For companies and teams

If you're a small team who wants to eat together without going out, we can prepare individual boxes for everyone or one dish to share. For groups of ten or more it usually costs less than going out for a set lunch.

It also works for organisations with volunteers and for long working days where there's no time for full catering.

Questions about the service

Can I order for just one day?

Yes. Some people order five a week and some order one. There's no commitment.

Can I bring my own container?

Yes, and we'd rather you did: it saves packaging and the food often travels better.

How much notice do you need?

The day before is enough. For a whole team, a couple of days is better.

Tell us what you need

Call or email us. We reply with a real proposal and a fixed price, with no obligation and no endless forms.
