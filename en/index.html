We cook every day as if we were cooking for our own

We are a workers' co-operative in La Sagrera, Barcelona. We run a canteen, catering, cooking workshops and a community kitchen using sustainable produce bought straight from the farmers. And we use food to explain where it comes from.

How to join in

Food as culture, not as a chore

We want to build a culture of food through what children eat every day.

The co-operative's mission, written in 2014 and still current

The children set the table, serve themselves and clear up. Everything gets tasted; nothing gets forced. Supervision is not policing: it is an adult sitting down, eating the same food, answering why there is sweet potato in November and none in June.

  • Cooked in our own kitchen the same day, nothing pre-cooked or chilled
  • Mostly vegetarian menus, published every month
  • Adapted for allergies, intolerances and vegan diets
  • Projects like the advent calendar, where each day reveals a winter vegetable

Questions we get a lot

Do you cater outside Sant Andreu?

Yes. We serve the whole of Barcelona and the surrounding Barcelonès municipalities. For longer distances we assess it based on the size and timing of the event.

What is the minimum number of guests for catering?

We work from 10 guests upwards. Below that we can prepare food for you to collect from the kitchen.

Can you adapt menus for allergies and intolerances?

Yes. We prepare gluten-free, dairy-free, nut-free, vegetarian and vegan menus. We need to know in advance so we can organise the kitchen and avoid cross-contamination.

How do you know the produce is sustainable?

We buy directly from farmers and co-operatives in the region, dealing with them personally. We can always tell you where something came from and who grew it.

Are you a for-profit business?

No. We are a non-profit social-purpose workers' co-operative, and we hold the Catalan certification for socially committed businesses.
