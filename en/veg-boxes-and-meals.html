Veg boxes and meals delivered

Build-your-own boxes of farm-direct fruit and vegetables, and daily meals for older people cooked the same morning in our kitchen in La Sagrera.

Call us Email us

The build-your-own box

Most sustainable box schemes send you whatever they have. Ours you assemble yourself: you choose what you want and how much, from whatever is available that week. If you don't like turnips, no turnips will arrive.

  • Seasonal fruit and vegetables from growers across Catalonia
  • Contents and quantities chosen by you each week
  • Collect from us or have it delivered locally
  • No commitment: if a week doesn't suit, simply don't order

Meals for older people

Daily meals cooked the same morning, for older people living alone or who no longer want or are able to cook every day. No industrial trays and no food reheated three times.

  • Adapted textures and temperatures where needed
  • Low salt and adaptable to medical diets
  • Starter, main and fresh fruit for dessert
  • Collection or delivery depending on the case

This is one of the services where it shows most clearly that we are a social-purpose co-operative: we do it because it is needed, not because it is the most profitable thing.

Questions about boxes and meals

Is there a subscription or minimum commitment?

No. Order a box the weeks that suit you and leave it at that.

Can I choose what goes in?

Yes, that's the difference. We send you what's available and you put the box together.

Can meals be adapted to a medical diet?

Yes, if you send us the guidance. We work without salt, with modified textures and with other common restrictions.

Do you deliver?

Locally, yes. Outside that area it's easier for you to collect.

Can meals for older people be booked on individual days?

Yes. Some people order five a week and some order two.

Tell us what you need

Call or email us. We reply with a real proposal and a fixed price, with no obligation and no endless forms.
